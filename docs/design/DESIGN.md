## Brand & Style

The brand personality is grounded in calm reassurance, warmth, emotional safety, and quiet clarity. Designed for individuals navigating daily stress, anxiety, emotional self-reflection, and mental maintenance, the interface feels like an unhurried sanctuary rather than a clinical dashboard or an over-gamified productivity tracker. 

The aesthetic marries **Minimalism** with **Gentle Organic Tactility**. Clean layouts, generous negative space, and breathable typographic pacing prevent cognitive overload. Soft, comforting surface treatments, pill-shaped geometry, and subtle atmospheric layering replace harsh lines and dense information displays. The overall tone is encouraging, non-judgmental, protective, and genuinely human.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional landscape rooted in tranquil sky blues, restorative sage greens, and soothing misty lavenders, balanced by a warm slate typographic structure and soft off-white canvas tones.

- **Primary (`#3A6B88`)**: A deep tranquil ice/sky blue providing grounded visual anchor points for active states, key interactive buttons, and primary progress indicators. Tints include `#E8F1F5` (whisper sky base) and `#D2E4EC` (soft surface tint).
- **Secondary (`#4D7856`)**: A grounded sage green reserved for grounding exercises, positive reinforcement, stability metrics, and mindful achievements. Supporting tints are `#E8EFE9` and `#C5D8C7`.
- **Tertiary (`#7D6B90`)**: A misty lavender applied to reflection prompts, bedtime or evening rituals, journal categorization, and subtle privacy or security highlights. Tinted with `#F0EDF5`.
- **Neutral (`#2C3539`)**: Warm slate providing high legibility without the jarring starkness of pure black. Paired with secondary text tone `#606E75` for metadata, timestamps, and placeholder guidance.
- **Canvas & Surfaces**: The base canvas is `#FAFBF9` (warm linen off-white), accompanied by `#FFFFFF` for floating interactive cards and nested container tiles.
- **Semantic Accents**: Soft amber (`#D98E52`) for gentle notices and muted rose (`#C26767`) for sensitive emotional check-in cues.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans delivers geometric clarity blended with warm, open counters and subtle humanist curves. This provides welcoming legibility across both expressive check-in dialogues and dense journal entries.

- **Rhythm & Line Length**: Body text maintains a relaxed 1.5x to 1.6x line-height ratio to prevent visual strain. Paragraph widths are capped at 60 characters on wider mobile/tablet viewports.
- **Hierarchy Tiers**: Headlines prioritize medium and semibold weights over ultra-bold display styles to preserve a gentle atmosphere.
- **Privacy Labels**: Discrete security badges and sensitive metadata leverage `label-sm` set in medium tracking to maintain legibility at compact dimensions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a fluid single-column model on mobile devices that expands gracefully into 4 columns for tablets and 8 to 12 responsive columns for wider landscape environments. Generous vertical breathing room replaces dense lists.

- **Grid & Gutters**: Mobile screens use a fluid container bounded by `margin` (20px / 1.25rem) and standard internal component gaps scaled to 16px (`space-md`). Larger screens increase outer gutters to 24px and section paddings to `space-xxl` (56px / 3.5rem).
- **Rhythm**: Spacing between unrelated thematic groups (such as transitioning from an active breathing prompt to historical mood logs) utilizes `space-xl` (36px / 2.25rem) or `space-xxl` to create distinct mental transitions.
- **Safe Margins**: Dynamic bottom padding accounts for navigation bars and the iOS home indicator, ensuring actionable surfaces never feel constrained against physical device boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Layers** paired with **Ambient Shadows** and **Ultra-Subtle Outlines**, avoiding stark drops or heavy shadows.

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#FAFBF9`, warm unadorned ground.
  - **Level 1 (Card & Section Surfaces)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` or pale pastel washes (`#E8F1F5`, `#E8EFE9`, `#F0EDF5`) with a 0.5px or 1px border colored at `rgba(44, 53, 57, 0.06)`.
  - **Level 2 (Floating Modals & Sheets)**: `#FFFFFF` paired with an ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(44, 53, 57, 0.05), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(44, 53, 57, 0.03)`.
- **Glassmorphism Elements**: Sticky top headers and bottom navigation docks utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` over an 85% transparent `#FAFBF9` wash, keeping navigation anchored while content glides gently underneath.

## Shapes

The design uses a generous, organic shape language dominated by continuous pill silhouettes and large rounded curves (`roundedness: 3`).

- **Pill Containers**: Interactive buttons, mood chips, filter pills, and audio session scrubber controls feature fully rounded radii (9999px / pill).
- **Card Enclosures**: Standard card containers feature a 24px (`rounded-2xl`) to 32px (`rounded-3xl`) radius, removing sharp visual corners to foster feelings of ease and calm.
- **Dialogs & Drawers**: Bottom sheets and expressive interactive modals employ a 32px radius along top edges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Full pill shape with a `#3A6B88` background, `#FFFFFF` typography (`label-lg`), and 14px vertical by 24px horizontal padding. Hover/active states blend softly into a darkened `#2E566D` tone without abrupt transitions.
- **Secondary**: Pill shape with `#E8F1F5` fill, `#3A6B88` text, and zero border. Active state shifts softly to `#D2E4EC`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent fill with `#606E75` label text, switching to `#2C3539` on press.

### Mood & Category Chips
- Pill-shaped items with 8px vertical and 16px horizontal padding.
- Unselected state uses `#FFFFFF` fill with an ultra-soft border (`rgba(44, 53, 57, 0.08)`) and `#606E75` text.
- Selected state adopts contextual tinted surfaces (e.g., `#E8EFE9` for calm, `#F0EDF5` for mindful) with matching text colors (`#4D7856` or `#7D6B90`) and an internal micro-check or subtle halo.

### Cards & Container Panels
- Floating white (`#FFFFFF`) containers with 24px (`rounded-2xl`) or 32px (`rounded-3xl`) curvature, 20px internal padding, and a hairline border (`rgba(44, 53, 57, 0.05)`).
- Card headers pair an unobtrusive icon container (40x40px circle or soft square) with concise `headline-sm` titles and comfortable spacing.

### Lists
- Floating separated card rows rather than cramped dividing lines. Each row sits on `#FFFFFF` with a 16px radius, separated by `space-sm` (8px). 
- Right-aligned chevrons and metadata employ `#606E75` at reduced opacity.

### Input Fields & Journal Entries
- Text inputs utilize an inset pill shape (for single lines) or a 20px rounded container (for journal reflection blocks).
- Surfaces use `#FFFFFF` or `#F7F9F6` with an inner hairline border. On focus, the field transitions to a 1.5px `#3A6B88` ring with a 4px diffused ice-blue outer glow (`rgba(58, 107, 136, 0.12)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Checkboxes use 20px rounded squares (`rounded-md`); radios use pure 20px circles.
- Unchecked state uses a 1.5px `#C5D8C7` or `#606E75` perimeter with empty fill. Checked state fills smoothly with `#4D7856` (check mark) or `#3A6B88` (radio center dot) in white.

### Discreet Privacy Indicators
- Lock and shield indicators accompany private reflections and encrypted notes.
- Styled as compact pill badges (`label-sm`) with `#F0EDF5` background, `#7D6B90` text, and a miniature 12px padlock glyph indicating local end-to-end encryption.
- Positioned subtly in header sub-bars or card corners without visual alarm.

### Guided Breathing / Session Wheel
- Circular, smooth-scaling SVG elements surrounded by concentric atmospheric rings utilizing primary and secondary color tints (`rgba(58, 107, 136, 0.08)` and `rgba(77, 120, 86, 0.1)`).
- Animations run on gentle ease-in-out curves (4000ms breathing cycles) to support somatic pacing.
